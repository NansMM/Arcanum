Custom sounds for Arcanum
=========================

Arcanum plays game sounds. You can pick other ones in the settings (/arc), or drop your own .ogg files
here (they play as long as the proc's sound is set to "Default"):

  clear.ogg     Clearcasting
  barrage.ogg   Missile Barrage

WoW only sees new files after a full restart of the game (a /reload is not enough), and an Arcanum update
may wipe this folder: keep a copy of yours.
